Privacy

The short version: the text you mask in Hushpaste is processed in your browser. We never receive it.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

What stays on your device

  • Text you paste into the free tool is detected, masked and restored by JavaScript running on this page. It is not sent to us or to any other server.
  • The map between placeholders and real values lives in the open tab and disappears when you close it.
  • We do not use accounts, and no third-party JavaScript runs on this site. We do count page visits, and we do it without script: a GoatCounter tracking pixel, a 1×1 image request that records only which page was opened and when. It runs no JavaScript, sets no cookies, cannot read the page, and never sees anything you paste.
  • The pages carry a Content Security Policy that allows two outgoing requests and nothing else: the waitlist form to Formspree, and the visit-counting image to GoatCounter. Third-party scripts remain blocked outright.

What we collect

  • If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you type into the form. We use it only to send launch news about Hushpaste and the AI Paste Safety Kit.
  • We do not sell your email address or share it for advertising.

Services this site uses

  • Formspree receives and stores waitlist submissions on our behalf.
  • GoatCounter counts page visits. Each visit sends the page address, the page title and the time. As with any image request, your browser also sends its IP address and User-Agent; GoatCounter uses these to derive the browser and the country, holds them in memory for up to eight hours to recognise a repeat visit, and does not store IP addresses in its database. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no script on this site, and cannot read page content or anything you paste.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, it may log technical request data such as IP address.
  • Fonts are served from this site itself. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to delete your email address at any time. Reply to any email we send you with the word "delete" and we will remove it.
  • You can use the free tool without giving us any personal data.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
